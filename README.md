# Vector Chem

A standalone, publication-grade STEM vector diagram studio for chemistry and laboratory sciences. Built with **React 19**, **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Vite**.

- **Live URL**: [https://vector-chem.vercel.app](https://vector-chem.vercel.app)
- **Suite Family**: [Vector Physics](https://vector-physics.vercel.app) · [Vector Math](https://vector-math.vercel.app)

---

## Generators Included

1. **Particulate Matter Diagrams**: Solid, liquid, and gas particle arrangements, pure elements, diatomic molecules, compounds, and mixtures with velocity trails.
2. **Lewis Dot Structures**: Central atoms, single/double/triple bonds, lone pairs, polyatomic ion brackets, and formal charge callouts.
3. **Bohr-Rutherford Atomic Models**: Concentric electron energy shells (K, L, M, N), proton and neutron counts, and valence electron distributions.
4. **Phase Change Heating & Cooling Curves**: Temperature vs heat added curves with solid, liquid, gas regions, melting and boiling plateaus, and phase labels.
5. **Acid-Base Titration Curves**: Strong/weak acid-base titration curves, equivalence point marker, buffer region, half-equivalence pH, and indicators.
6. **Lab Glassware Meniscus Reading**: Graduated cylinders, burets, and thermometers with calibrated tick subdivisions and realistic curved meniscus reading.
7. **Galvanic & Voltaic Cells**: Two-half-cell electrochemical apparatus with anode/cathode electrodes, salt bridge, electron flow arrow, and voltmeter.

---

## Features
- **3× High-DPI PNG Copying**: Directly rasterizes SVG onto offscreen canvas and writes to clipboard.
- **Clean SVG Downloads**: Sanitized XML for publication or editing.
- **Stateless Sharing**: Encodes state into URL search parameters.
- **Print Styles**: Formatted for classroom handouts, AP Chemistry quizzes, and lab worksheets.
